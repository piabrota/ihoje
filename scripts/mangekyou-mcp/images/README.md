# The Mangekyou Sharingan

The Mangekyou Sharingan (万華鏡写輪眼, "Kaleidoscope Copy Wheel Eye") is an advanced form of the Sharingan that appears in the Naruto manga and anime series. It is a powerful ocular jutsu possessed by members of the Uchiha clan.

## Overview

The Mangekyou Sharingan is awakened by an Uchiha who has already mastered the Sharingan when they experience intense emotional trauma, typically the death of someone very close to them. Each Mangekyou Sharingan has a unique pattern that differs from user to user.

## Abilities

The Mangekyou Sharingan grants its users several powerful abilities, though specific techniques vary between users:

1. **Amaterasu (天照)**: Creates black flames at the focal point of the user's vision that cannot be extinguished and burn for seven days and nights. These flames burn through almost anything.

2. **Tsukuyomi (月読)**: An extremely powerful genjutsu that traps opponents in an illusionary world where the caster controls time, space, and mass. It can make seconds seem like days to those trapped in its effects.

3. **Susanoo (須佐能乎)**: A gigantic, spectral warrior that surrounds and protects the user. It can be manifested in stages, from a skeletal form to a complete humanoid.

4. **Kamui (神威)**: Allows the user to transfer targets to and from another dimension. Can be used for teleportation, intangibility, or ranged attacks.

## Notable Users

- **Itachi Uchiha**: Possessed Tsukuyomi and Amaterasu, which combined allowed him to use Susanoo.
- **Sasuke Uchiha**: Possessed Amaterasu and could control its flames with Kagutsuchi. Could also use Susanoo.
- **Obito Uchiha**: Possessed Kamui in his right eye, allowing him to become intangible and teleport.
- **Kakashi Hatake**: Temporarily possessed Kamui in his left eye (transplanted from Obito), allowing him to teleport targets at a distance.
- **Madara Uchiha**: Possessed extremely powerful versions of the common Mangekyou abilities.
- **Shisui Uchiha**: Possessed Kotoamatsukami, the ultimate genjutsu that could control others without them being aware.

## Drawbacks

The use of the Mangekyou Sharingan causes deterioration of the user's eyesight, eventually leading to blindness with continued use. The only way to restore sight permanently is to transplant the Mangekyou Sharingan of a close blood relative, resulting in the Eternal Mangekyou Sharingan.
