# Mangekyou Sharingan Patterns

The Mangekyou Sharingan has unique patterns for each user. Here are the patterns of notable Mangekyou Sharingan users from the Naruto series:

## Itachi Uchiha
Itachi's Mangekyou Sharingan resembles a three-pronged pinwheel (三勾玉, "three-tomoe"). His right eye specializes in casting Amaterasu, while his left eye casts Tsukuyomi. With both eyes, he can manifest Susanoo.

## Sasuke Uchiha
Sasuke's Mangekyou Sharingan has a pattern similar to a six-pointed star or atom. His right eye can cast and control Amaterasu (called Kagutsuchi when controlled), and his left eye can cast Amaterasu itself. Like Itachi, he can manifest Susanoo with both eyes.

## Obito Uchiha
Obito's Mangekyou Sharingan has a pattern that resembles a three-pronged pinwheel, but it's more stretched out than Itachi's. His right eye possesses the ability Kamui, allowing him to become intangible and teleport himself.

## Kakashi Hatake
Kakashi possessed Obito's left eye, which had the same pattern as Obito's. This eye also possessed Kamui, but instead of becoming intangible, Kakashi's version could teleport targets at a distance.

## Madara Uchiha
Madara's Mangekyou Sharingan has a pattern similar to a windmill with straight tomoe instead of curved ones. His abilities include casting powerful genjutsu, Susanoo, and possibly other unknown abilities.

## Shisui Uchiha
Shisui's Mangekyou Sharingan has a pattern resembling a four-pointed pinwheel. His eyes possessed the ability Kotoamatsukami, the most powerful genjutsu in the series, capable of controlling targets without them ever realizing they were under its influence.

## Izuna Uchiha
Izuna's Mangekyou Sharingan (Madara's younger brother) resembled a three-pointed pinwheel similar to Itachi's, but rotated differently. His specific abilities were never explicitly stated.

## Fugaku Uchiha (Anime Only)
In the anime, Sasuke and Itachi's father Fugaku was shown to have awakened the Mangekyou Sharingan. It resembled three curved lines stretching from the pupil. His abilities were never shown.

## Indra Otsutsuki (Ancestor)
As the ancestor of the Uchiha clan, Indra was the first to awaken the Sharingan, and later the Mangekyou Sharingan. His pattern resembled a four-pointed star. His abilities included Perfect Susanoo.

## Shin Uchiha (Non-Uchiha)
A non-Uchiha who had Sharingan implanted in various parts of his body. His Mangekyou Sharingan resembled a cross between a normal Sharingan and a flower. His ability allowed him to manipulate weapons telepathically.

## Eternal Mangekyou Sharingan

The Eternal Mangekyou Sharingan is achieved when an Uchiha with the Mangekyou Sharingan takes the eyes of a close blood relative who also has the Mangekyou Sharingan. This prevents the blindness that comes with regular Mangekyou Sharingan use.

Notable users:
- Madara Uchiha (transplanted his brother Izuna's eyes)
- Sasuke Uchiha (transplanted his brother Itachi's eyes)

The pattern of the Eternal Mangekyou Sharingan is a fusion of both the user's original pattern and the transplanted eyes' pattern.
